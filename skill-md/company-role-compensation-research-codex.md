# Company, Role, and Compensation Research

Read `references/evidence-standard.md`, then research current sources. If web access is unavailable, request URLs or documents and state the limitation.

## Source ladder

1. Direct job posting and official employer, leadership, benefits, and press pages.
2. SEC EDGAR for public-company filings; official WARN and labor-department sources for material layoffs.
3. BLS OEWS for occupation/location wages and O*NET for standardized tasks and skills.
4. Reputable dated reporting.
5. Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Indeed, Blind, and similar self-reported sources as secondary evidence only.

## Output

- Role facts: scope, requirements, location eligibility, posted compensation, and unanswered questions.
- Company facts: business model, leadership, financial or funding signals, material events, and hiring pattern.
- Culture evidence: official claims separated from employee sentiment; summarize patterns without presenting anecdotes as fact.
- Compensation: base, bonus mechanics, equity type and vesting, benefits, insurance, retirement match, leave, and location effects.
- Interviewer notes: public professional information only; no sensitive-person inference.
- Risk register: evidence, date, confidence, contradictions, and candidate questions.
- Bottom line: fit evidence and tradeoffs, never a fabricated probability.

Every material claim needs a nearby link and source date. Mark agent synthesis explicitly as inference. Require human approval before external action.
